# Executive Business Insights
## Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

**Analysis Period:** 2014–2017  
**Dataset:** Sample Superstore  
**Records Analyzed:** 9,994

---

### Insight 1: Furniture Generates Strong Revenue but Weak Profitability

**Key Findings**

- Furniture generated approximately **$742,000 in sales**.
- Furniture produced only **$18,451 in profit**.
- Its profit margin was **2.49%**, compared with **17.40% for Technology** and **17.04% for Office Supplies**.
- Within Furniture, the **Tables** subcategory recorded an overall loss of approximately **$17,725**.

**Business Implication**

Furniture contributes substantial revenue but delivers relatively little profit. The category's low margin indicates a potential profitability problem that deserves further investigation.

**Recommended Actions**

1. Investigate loss-making Furniture products, particularly Tables.
2. Review discount patterns and their association with profit margins.
3. Evaluate product-level pricing and cost structures before changing promotional strategies.

**Analytical Note**

These findings identify profitability patterns and potential areas for improvement. Additional analysis is required to determine the underlying causes of low margins.
