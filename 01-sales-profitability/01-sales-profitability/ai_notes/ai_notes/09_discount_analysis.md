# Discount Impact Analysis

## Objective

Analyze the relationship between discount levels and profitability.

## Findings

| Discount Level | Profitability |
|---|---|
|0%-20%|Positive profit margins|
|30%+|Negative profit margins|

## Key Insights

- No-discount transactions generated the highest profitability.
- Moderate discounts maintained positive margins.
- Discounts above 30% consistently resulted in losses.
- Extreme discounts created significant profitability erosion.

## Business Recommendation

Review discount approval policies and establish discount thresholds to protect profit margins.

AI was used to help identify patterns and generate investigation hypotheses.

All conclusions were validated through aggregated profitability calculations.
