# Customer Segment Profitability Analysis

## Objective

Analyze customer segments to identify revenue contribution and profitability efficiency.

---

## Segment Performance

| Segment | Sales | Profit | Profit Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
|Consumer|$1.16M|$134.1K|11.55%|
|Corporate|$706.1K|$92K|13.03%|
|Home Office|$429.7K|$60.3K|14.03%|

---

## Key Findings

- Consumer is the largest revenue and profit contributor.
- Consumer generates approximately half of total revenue but has the lowest profit margin among segments.
- Home Office has the highest profit margin despite having lower sales volume.
- Corporate provides a balance between revenue scale and profitability.

---

## Business Insights

The Consumer segment drives business volume, but higher-margin segments such as Home Office demonstrate stronger profitability efficiency.

The company should maintain Consumer customer volume while exploring opportunities to grow higher-margin customer segments.

---
