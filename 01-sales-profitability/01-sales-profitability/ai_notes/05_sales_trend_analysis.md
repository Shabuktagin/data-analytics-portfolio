# Sales Trend Analysis

## Objective

Analyze yearly sales, profit, and profitability trends.

## Yearly Performance

| Year | Sales | Profit | Profit Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
|2014|$484K|$49.5K|10.23%|
|2015|$470.5K|$61.6K|13.10%|
|2016|$609.2K|$81.8K|13.43%|
|2017|$733.2K|$93.4K|12.74%|

## Key Findings

- Sales increased approximately 51% from 2014 to 2017.
- Profit increased every year.
- 2016 achieved the highest profit margin (13.43%).
- 2017 achieved the highest sales but experienced slight margin decline.

## Business Hypothesis

The 2017 margin decline may be related to:
- increased discounting
- lower-margin product categories
- changes in product mix

Further analysis will investigate these factors.
