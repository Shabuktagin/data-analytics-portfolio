Establish the overall business performance baseline before performing deeper analysis.

The executive KPI layer provides a high-level view of revenue, profitability, customer activity, discount strategy, and operational performance.

# Business Interpretation

## Revenue Performance

The dataset represents 5,009 unique orders generating approximately $2.3 million in sales.

This provides the baseline for understanding revenue drivers across:

- Products
- Categories
- Regions
- Customer segments

---

## Profitability Performance

The business generated approximately $286K in profit with an overall profit margin of 12.47%.

This means that for every $100 in revenue, approximately $12.47 was retained as profit.

Future analysis will investigate:

- Which products generate strong margins
- Which products create losses
- How discounts affect profitability

---

## Discount Strategy

The average discount applied was 15.62%.

However, earlier data investigation identified discounts ranging up to 80%.

Further analysis is required to determine whether:

- Higher discounts increase sales volume
- Excessive discounts reduce profitability

---

## Operational Performance

Average shipping time:

3.96 days

This metric will be used as a baseline for analyzing:

- Shipping mode performance
- Regional delivery differences
- Operational efficiency

# Initial Business Questions Generated

The KPI analysis leads to further investigation:

1. Which categories generate the highest sales and profit?
2. Which regions are most profitable?
3. Which products have high sales but low profitability?
4. How does discount level impact profit margin?
5. Which customer segments contribute the most value?
6. Are sales and profits improving over time?
