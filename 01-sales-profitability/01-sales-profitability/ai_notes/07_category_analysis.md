# Category Profitability Analysis

## Objective

Analyze product categories to identify revenue and profitability drivers.

## Category Performance

| Category | Sales | Profit | Profit Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
|Furniture|$742K|$18.5K|2.49%|
|Office Supplies|$719K|$122.5K|17.04%|
|Technology|$836K|$145.5K|17.40%|

## Key Findings

- Technology is the strongest category by sales, profit, and margin.
- Office Supplies generates strong profitability despite lower sales volume.
- Furniture generates high revenue but has very low profitability.

## Business Hypothesis

Furniture profitability issues may be related to:
- discount strategy
- product-level losses
- sub-category performance

Further analysis is required.
